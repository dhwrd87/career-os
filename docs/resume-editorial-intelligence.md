# Resume Editorial Intelligence V1

Career OS inserts an evidence-bound editorial layer between profile/role routing and document composition. `ResumeStorylineV1` ranks canonical evidence as anchor, supporting, context, optional, or distracting. `ResumeBulletCandidateV1` turns completed, supported evidence into concise recruiter copy while preserving evidence IDs, metric references, verification state, visibility, confidence, and source lineage.

`PublicDisplayPolicyV1` governs recruiter-facing education, credentials, skills, metrics, and visibility. Private or restricted evidence is excluded. Credentials that are not in an allowed completion and verification state stay in internal content/manifests but are not rendered with internal governance labels. Unknown project dates are omitted rather than displayed as missing.

`ResumeEditorialQualityV1` evaluates role relevance, story coherence, evidence strength, outcome density, metric/scale quality, recruiter clarity, concision, human voice, lane differentiation, system-artifact leakage, and semantic duplication. Truth, provenance, credential state, and metric safety remain separate hard gates. Editorial scoring can never override them.

Lane differentiation is evidence-routing, not headline replacement: role-family capabilities affect evidence ranking, project order, selected skill groups, and summary strategy. The public resume is a presentation derivative. Canonical career records remain provenance-rich internal authorities and are never replaced by recruiter copy.

The [v0.4.2 actual-output review](recruiter-copy-review-0.4.2.md) documents the current copy repairs and evidence. Scores are internal heuristic checks, not independent human ratings, competitor preference or hiring-outcome predictions.
