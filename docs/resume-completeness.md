# Resume completeness and anti-overcompression

`ResumeCompletenessV1` is independent from job relevance. A build must be both focused on the supplied job and sufficiently complete to establish workplace credibility, career continuity, technical preparation, and target direction.

Evidence tiers are `ANCHOR`, `SUPPORTING`, `TRANSFERABLE_PROFESSIONAL`, `FOUNDATIONAL`, `OPTIONAL`, and `IRRELEVANT`. Direct job matches lead. Distinct supporting projects, transferable professional work, relevant education, publicly permitted credentials, and candidate-confirmed languages remain available to the document when they strengthen the story. Optional evidence uses remaining space; irrelevant or private evidence is omitted.

For career changers, visible professional roles are retained as full, compact, or minimal chronology records. Project selection normally preserves up to four distinct projects, with at least three when three strong projects exist. This is a breadth floor, not permission to repeat the same evidence.

`UNUSED_RELEVANT_EVIDENCE` records every positively ranked item omitted for `SPACE`, `REDUNDANT`, `WEAK_EVIDENCE`, `PRIVACY`, `LOW_PRIORITY`, `QUALIFICATION_BOUNDARY`, or `OTHER`. A visibly underfilled page with unused relevant evidence fails `UNDERFILLED_RESUME`; it may not be repaired by inflated typography or decorative whitespace.
