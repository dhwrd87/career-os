# Find your next role

A useful search starts with two things: a fair picture of what you can do and a clear idea of what matters in your next job. Keep your career profile up to date, then tell your assistant what you want to explore.

## Find actual openings

Ask, “Find jobs that fit my experience and goals.” The assistant uses your work history, skills, qualifications and preferences. It can read supported employer boards or bring in current postings from public search. It needs a real source; it will not invent an opening or pretend to have searched every job board.

It reviews the full job requirements, removes repeated listings and compares the jobs with your profile. Required qualifications are kept separate from employer preferences. Missing information is shown as something to check.

## Decide where to focus

**Ready Now** jobs fit the requirements checked so far, have current posting details and fit your search preferences. You still review the employer, pay, working arrangements and application before applying.

**Stretch Roles** need some development or clarification. You might have useful transferable skills while still lacking a required qualification. A question about a posting is not proof that you lack a skill.

**Dream Roles** are longer-term goals you have named. A real posting helps us see what the role asks for and what steps may help you get there. A high gap score alone does not make a job your dream role.

## Explore other titles

Ask, “What other roles could I try?” The suggestions use your stated goals, actual work history and relevant requirements in checked postings. Each should explain what carries over and what still needs attention. A suggested title is an idea to explore, not a verified vacancy by itself.

## Prepare your resumes

Ask, “Make resumes for my top three matches,” or name the jobs you want. Each suitable job gets its own tailored draft using your approved facts and work examples. The checks protect accuracy and layout. They do not predict whether an employer will respond.

If required qualifications, source details or content checks are missing, the draft is held with an explanation. Nothing is sent. Review every resume before using it.

## Work toward a stretch or dream role

Ask, “What would help me reach this role?” The plan uses that job's actual requirements, rather than mixing in gaps from unrelated roles. It compares suitable training, certificates, projects and experience-building steps with your current knowledge, available time, budget and subscriptions.

Start with a manageable step. Keep a piece of work that shows what you learned, then review any new skill or actual award before adding it to your profile. Compare the role again after approved changes. A course does not replace required professional experience or a degree. See [your learning plan](learning-path-intelligence.md).
