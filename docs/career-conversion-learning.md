# Career conversion learning

Career OS 0.3.17 adds candidate-specific, descriptive learning over real `OpportunityV1`, `ApplicationEventV1`, qualification, resume-lineage, and employer-need records. Packet preparation, unsubmitted drafts, missing responses, and guessed historical activity never enter a cohort.

`CareerConversionCohortV1` records the exact applications, opportunities, role families, policy versions, market window, filters, known and unknown outcome counts, operational maturity, and a deterministic hash. Maturity is configurable and considers sample count, known outcomes, outcome diversity, comparability, elapsed time, and staleness. It is not a statistical-significance claim.

`ConversionCausalSafeguardV1` permits association language only. Resume, qualification, skill, and policy variants are not described as causes without a genuinely causal design. Small, stale, mixed, or confounded cohorts block strong recommendations. Development and course/credential records retain their descriptive observations but carry `recommendation_allowed: false` until the cohort reaches `DEVELOPING` or `MATURE`.

Career-capital, recurring-gap, development-opportunity, course/credential ROI, and observed-market projections remain candidate-specific. They do not replace broad market research and cannot become generic package policy without explicitly consented anonymized evidence.

`LearningPromotionProposalV1` is the only route from an observation toward policy review. Proposals begin `PENDING`; the package has no automatic policy mutation path. Approval changes proposal state only and does not apply a policy.
