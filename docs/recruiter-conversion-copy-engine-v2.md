# Recruiter Conversion Copy Engine V2

Career OS 0.3.21 separates internal truth controls from public recruiter prose. Canonical evidence, EmployerNeedTraceV1, RoleFactSetV1, and MaterialEvidenceLossV1 continue to define what may be claimed. RecruiterConversionStrategyV2 defines positioning, proof, credibility, differentiation, and the intended recruiter takeaway before copy is composed.

Public resume copy then passes through evidence-shaped summary and bullet construction, PublicCopyBoundaryV1, natural-language and rhythm checks, RecruiterCopyQualityV2, and—for targeted resumes—TargetedEditorialRegressionV1. Qualification gaps, evidence-verification language, professional-equivalence cautions, and governance rationale remain internal. If evidence cannot support confident wording, the compiler uses simpler factual language rather than aspiration or disclaimers.

The quality contract scores positioning, specificity, professional confidence, employer relevance, career story, human voice, concision, evidence density, differentiation, readability, persuasiveness, and system-language absence. Production copy targets 9.3/10 without weakening truth, provenance, ATS, family inheritance, or public/private boundaries.
