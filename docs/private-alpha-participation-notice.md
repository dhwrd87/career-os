# Career OS private-alpha participation notice

Career OS stores the career facts, preferences, evidence metadata, workflow state, and generated career artifacts that you deliberately provide or approve. Its workspace is local/private to the participant unless the participant independently chooses another storage location.

Career OS may generate a structured career profile, role-family strategy, qualification analysis, resume, validation reports, and a review-ready application packet. Imported resumes, notes, or certificates create proposed records first. They do not become canonical until validation, clarification, review, and explicit approval.

The private alpha does not automatically submit applications, send messages, edit public profiles, schedule interviews, negotiate compensation, or accept offers. External-action adapters remain disabled.

Diagnostic reporting is redacted by default. Product feedback may retain workflow category, severity, operator observation, and resolution without retaining names, employers, dates, metrics, credentials, source documents, or other personal career facts. Raw participant data must not enter the package repository, synthetic fixtures, generic documentation, or public issue trackers.

Participants can create a portable export containing canonical structured data, history, relevant workflow metadata, and generated artifacts. Source documents are distinguished and excluded by default. Generated state can be reset through a recoverable archive. Workspace deletion requires the exact workspace ID and an explicit confirmation phrase; the implemented local deletion path moves the workspace to a participant-selected archive so it remains recoverable.

Participation is voluntary. The operator must record affirmative consent before ingesting personal career material and must honor a request to stop, export, archive, or delete the local workspace.
