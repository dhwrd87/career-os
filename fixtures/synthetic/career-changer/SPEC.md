# Fixture B — Mid-Career Career Changer

## Fixture purpose

Exercise a truthful career-change route where deep domain operations experience can support a new role family but does not equal direct experience under the target title.

## Fictional career summary

Priya Nordin worked for eight years in outpatient clinic administration and is moving toward software implementation coordination. Priya completed a recent configuration course, supported one nonprofit migration as a volunteer, and had a six-month caregiving gap. Any contact fixture will use `priya.nordin@example.invalid`.

## Employment structure

- Willowbend Family Clinic — Patient Services Coordinator, four years.
- Harborline Pediatrics Group — Scheduling and Intake Lead, four years, prior role.
- Six-month caregiving gap represented as an optional private timeline fact, not employment.

## Education

- Bachelor of Arts in Communications, completed.
- Continuing-education course in software configuration and implementation support.

## Skills

- Requirements discovery, workflow documentation, scheduling operations, staff training, issue triage, data cleanup, stakeholder communication, and basic configuration.
- Direct enterprise implementation ownership is not evidenced.

## Credentials

- Fictional Implementation Support Foundations certificate, completed.
- Privacy-awareness training, current.
- No project-management certification.

## Projects

- Volunteer migration for Open Harbor Literacy: mapped 1,200 fictional donor records, prepared validation steps, and trained two coordinators.
- Contribution was data preparation and user support; vendor selection and technical architecture were owned by others.

## Evidence complexities

- Clinic cycle-time improvement is documented, but causation is shared across a team.
- Volunteer project is real work but unpaid and must not be presented as formal employment.
- Target-role equivalence is a qualification judgment, not a factual title rewrite.

## Privacy cases

- Caregiving gap display is user-controlled.
- Patient and donor data may never be stored in fixture artifacts.
- Prior employer details are internal until the candidate approves public visibility.

## Target roles

- Implementation Coordinator
- Customer Onboarding Specialist
- Business Systems Support Specialist

## Expected clarification cases

- Whether a clinic improvement percentage is individually attributable.
- Whether the caregiving gap should appear in public materials.
- Preferred travel limit and hybrid/remote constraints.
- Compensation target currency and base-versus-total meaning.

## Expected schema edge cases

- Career gap with optional visibility.
- Volunteer project linked to evidence but not employment.
- Shared outcome with bounded ownership.
- Career-change intent and hard/soft qualification gaps.
- Target titles differ from every actual title.

## Expected future resume QA cases

- Two-page resume with a clear transition thesis.
- Actual titles remain intact while target-role relevance is explained.
- No claim of commercial software implementation ownership.
- Supported shared metrics retain contribution qualifiers.
- Interview stories distinguish operational expertise from new technical exposure.
