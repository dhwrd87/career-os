# Fixture C — Experienced Multi-Role Professional

## Fixture purpose

Exercise complex chronology, promotions, overlapping consulting, management and individual-contributor routes, credential states, confidential evidence, and page-pressure behavior.

## Fictional career summary

Elena Marwick has fifteen years across manufacturing operations, procurement, continuous improvement, and program delivery. Her history includes two promotions at one employer, a concurrent limited consulting engagement, people leadership, an expired credential, and international project work. Any contact fixture will use `elena.marwick@example.invalid`.

- Kestrel Home Goods — Operations Program Manager, promoted from Process Improvement Lead and Production Planner over nine years.
- Marble Coast Packaging — Procurement Analyst, four years, earlier role.
- North Quay Advisory — Independent Process Consultant, limited concurrent engagements over two years.
- Same-employer promotions remain separate chronological records with explicit parent-employer linkage.
