## A practical search routine

1. Approve your career profile and choose your career directions. Review the master resume and career-specific starting resumes.

2. Search current employer postings using these titles and your preferences. Save the full descriptions and distinguish the job page from its application form.

3. Compare required qualifications, prioritize Ready Now opportunities and prepare complete draft application packages.

4. Review each Start Here checklist, required questions and documents. Apply manually only when ready.

5. Record your applications, interviews and outcomes. Revisit the plan using what you learn.

6. Use Stretch Roles to choose focused development. Add Dream Roles only when they are goals you have chosen.
